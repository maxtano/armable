Privacy

Armable · Terms

Armable is a drone workbench that your own AI assistant can operate. This page says what it keeps and why.

What is stored

DataWhy
Email and a hashed password, or your Google account’s emailTo sign you in. The password itself is never stored. Try Armable without either: a guest bench needs no email, and is deleted after 30 days without a visit unless you save it.
Aircraft identity: firmware, version, board, craft nameTo show your aircraft and its history.
Changes, tests and their resultsSo you and your assistant can see what changed and when.
Every call your assistant makes, with what it sent and got backSo you can audit what it did. Large answers are cut to 8 KB.
A reading of battery, signal, GPS and attitude every 10 seconds while connectedTo show trends. Nothing finer is kept.
Shows, tunes, files and settings backups you saveBecause you asked to keep them.
Builds, part ratings and reviewsBuilds are yours. A build you share can be read by anyone with its link until you stop sharing. Ratings and reviews are shown to everyone without your name.
Feedback you sendWhat you write, the page you were on, your browser and firmware, and a picture of the drone view if you include one. Read by the people who make Armable.

What is not stored

Raw flight logs stay in your browser unless you save them. Continuous telemetry is not kept. Armable has no AI model and sends nothing to one; your assistant app does that under its own terms.

Who can see it

Only you, and assistants you connect. Each assistant sees only what its permissions allow, and you can disconnect it at any time. No data is sold or shared.

Deleting it

Export everything from your account at any time. Deleting your account deletes all of the above, including the tracking tables, at once.
